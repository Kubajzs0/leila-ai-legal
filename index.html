Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Leila AI Assistant, an automated messaging tool operated for the Instagram account @heyiamleila, handles information relating to conversations with that account.

Controller / operator: The owner and administrator of the Instagram account @heyiamleila (Leila AI Assistant project).
Privacy contact: cerny3050@gmail.com.

Automated responses: Some Instagram replies may be generated or sent by an AI assistant, not a person. You may ask for human assistance, request deletion, or stop interacting at any time.

Information involved

When someone messages the account, Meta may supply the sender's Instagram-scoped identifier, username, message text, timestamps, and relevant context (such as story replies or attachment metadata). Please avoid including passwords or sensitive personal data.

Purpose and legal basis

We use message information only to respond to user-initiated conversations, preserve brief context, prevent duplicate replies, troubleshoot errors, and handle privacy requests. Where applicable, processing is based on legitimate interests in responding to messages, subject to applicable legal requirements and rights to object.

AI and hosting

If automated messaging is enabled, relevant message excerpts and short conversation context may be shared with an external AI inference provider, currently planned to be GroqCloud, to draft answers. The system may also use hosting infrastructure for receiving messages and storing limited context. Meta/Instagram independently processes Instagram data under its own policies. We do not sell DM conversation data.

Retention

The planned application will limit stored conversational text and messaging identifiers to a rolling maximum of 30 days unless an earlier deletion request is received or legal obligations require otherwise. This policy will be updated if the final live implementation has different retention periods. Instagram may separately retain messages in its own systems.

International processing

Service providers may process data outside the sender's country. Appropriate contractual and other protections will be used where required.

Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, restriction, objection, or deletion of data held by this assistant. Contact cerny3050@gmail.com. Deletion instructions are available here.

Security

We intend to restrict access to message history and API credentials and use encrypted connections. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

Children

This assistant is not intended for children or people below the minimum age required to use Instagram in their jurisdiction.

Updates

This policy may change to reflect deployment details, provider changes, and applicable legal requirements. Updates will be posted on this page.

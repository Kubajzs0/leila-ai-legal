Data Deletion Instructions

If you have messaged @heyiamleila, you can request deletion of information associated with your conversations that is held by Leila AI Assistant.

How to request removal

  1. Email cerny3050@gmail.com with the subject Leila AI Data Deletion Request.
  2. State the Instagram username used to contact @heyiamleila. Do not send your password, access token, or sensitive documents.
  3. We may ask you for reasonable proof that you control the Instagram account before acting on a request.

What is removed?

On verified request, we will remove matching DM content and associated account identifiers from the assistant's own active database, to the extent permitted by law and technically feasible. We aim to respond and complete valid requests within 30 days, and will explain if no matching data is held.

What is not removed?

This process does not remove conversation copies managed independently by Instagram/Meta or external providers. Use Instagram's own privacy controls for data stored by Meta.
